3Grammar · will, may, might

Predictions about the future

will / won't: a prediction we feel certain about. It will rain tomorrow. She won't be here on time.

may / might: something is possible, but not certain. He may be hungry. They might not come.

probably: usually after will, but before won't. I'll probably be late. She probably won't come.

Questions: Will the exam be difficult? What do you think will happen? We do not usually make prediction questions with may/might.

Opinion phrases: I think we'll win. I don't think we'll lose.
